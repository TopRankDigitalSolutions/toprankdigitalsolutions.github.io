This policy explains what information TopRank Digital Solutions collects through this website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 15, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to information collected when you visit toprankdigitalsolutions.com, interact with the website, or contact TopRank Digital Solutions about our services. It does not govern websites or services operated independently by third parties.

Information we collect

We collect limited information needed to operate, secure, understand, and improve the website.

  • Usage information. Google Analytics may collect information about visits, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, referral source, and interactions with the website.
  • Technical information. Cloudflare and GitHub Pages may process IP addresses, request details, timestamps, and related logs to deliver and protect the website.
  • Information you provide. If you schedule a consultation or otherwise contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, company, and project context.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.
  • Understand website performance and how visitors find and use it.
  • Respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and evaluate potential engagements.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, users, and systems.

We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

We use service providers that process information on our behalf or under their own terms:

  • Google Analytics helps us measure website usage and performance.
  • Cloudflare provides content delivery, network security, and related website infrastructure.
  • GitHub Pages hosts and publishes the website.
  • Calendly handles consultation scheduling. Calendly is linked from this website but is not embedded in it. When you follow a Calendly link, Calendly's privacy policy and terms apply to the information you provide there.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to prevent harm, fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Cookies and analytics choices

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visitors and measure website activity. You can limit cookies through your browser settings. You may also use Google's Analytics opt-out tools or privacy controls where available.

Browser settings that block cookies may affect how some websites function. This website does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals because there is no consistent industry standard for interpreting them.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operational, contractual, security, and legal needs. Retention periods may vary by the type of information and the service provider processing it.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, email privacy@toprankdigitalsolutions.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our website, services, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date when changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this policy or our privacy practices, email privacy@toprankdigitalsolutions.com.
